OKLCH Explorer

oklch(L C H / A) — Lightness 0–100%, Chroma 0–0.4 (colourfulness), Hue 0–360°, Alpha 0–1. Click a padlock to link that slider across squares: locked sliders of the same channel move together, keeping their offsets.

Each wheel shows every hue and chroma at that square's lightness: the angle is hue and the distance from the centre is chroma (the edge is 0.4). The marker is the square's colour; drag it to change H and C. Outside the dark line are colours no screen can show; they're painted with the stand-in colour the browser substitutes, which is why they still change. Inside the white line fits in sRGB;
